# New Hire Onboarding Guide

## Before your first day
You will receive your laptop by courier at least 3 business days before your start date.
Complete the I-9 form and tax paperwork in the HR portal before day one.

## Week one
Day one starts with a 9:30 orientation session hosted by People Ops.
Your manager will assign you an onboarding buddy who meets with you daily during the first two weeks.
Complete the mandatory security awareness training within your first 5 business days.

## First 90 days
You will agree 30/60/90-day goals with your manager by the end of week two.
A formal check-in happens at day 90; it is a conversation about fit and support, not a performance review.
Probationary period: none. Acme does not use probation periods for full-time hires.

## Getting help
Questions about pay or benefits go to people@acme.example.
IT issues go to the #it-help channel or the IT portal; urgent laptop problems can be raised by phone at extension 4357.
