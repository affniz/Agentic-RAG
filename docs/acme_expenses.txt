Acme Corp Travel & Expense Policy

Meals: up to $75 per day while travelling domestically, $100 per day internationally.
Hotels: book through the corporate travel portal; nightly cap is $250 (or $350 in designated high-cost cities such as New York, London, and Tokyo).
Flights: economy class for flights under 6 hours; premium economy allowed for flights of 6 hours or longer.
Receipts: required for every expense over $25. Submit expenses within 30 days of the trip's end date.
Late submissions (over 60 days) need director approval.
