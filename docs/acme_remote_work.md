# Acme Corp Remote Work Guidelines

Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week with manager approval.
Fully remote arrangements require approval from both the manager and HR, and are reviewed annually.

## Equipment stipend
Remote employees receive a one-time home-office stipend of $750 and a monthly internet stipend of $50.

## Working from abroad
Working from another country is allowed for up to 20 business days per calendar year.
Longer stays need tax and legal review; submit the request at least 30 days before travel.
