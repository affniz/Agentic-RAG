# Acme Corp Benefits Guide

## Health insurance
Acme pays 90% of the medical premium for employees and 75% for dependents.
Coverage begins on the first day of the month after your start date.
Open enrollment runs every November 1-15; outside that window you can only change plans after a qualifying life event, reported within 30 days.

## Retirement
Acme matches 100% of 401(k) contributions up to 4% of salary, plus 50% of the next 2%.
The company match vests over 3 years: one third after each full year of service.

## Parental leave
Birthing parents receive 20 weeks of fully paid leave; non-birthing parents receive 12 weeks.
Parental leave can be taken in up to two blocks within 12 months of the birth or adoption.

## Learning budget
Every employee has a $1,500 annual learning budget for courses, books, and conferences.
Unused learning budget does not roll over to the next year.
Conference travel paid from the learning budget still follows the travel and expense policy.

## Wellness
A $60 monthly wellness stipend covers gym memberships, fitness classes, or mental-health apps.
Employees and their household members get 8 free counselling sessions per year through the employee assistance program.
