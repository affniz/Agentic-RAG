# Acme Corp Vacation Policy (2026)

## Accrual
Full-time employees accrue 1.75 vacation days per month (21 days per year).
Part-time employees accrue vacation pro rata based on scheduled hours.
Employees with 5 or more years of service accrue 2.0 days per month (24 days per year).

## Carry-over
Up to 5 unused days may be carried over into the next calendar year.
Carried-over days expire on March 31 if not used.

## Requesting time off
Requests of 3 days or more must be submitted at least 2 weeks in advance via the HR portal.
Requests during the December freeze (Dec 15 - Dec 31) require VP approval.
