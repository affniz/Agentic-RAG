# Safety Training Requirements

Required certifications by role. Intervals count from the date the certificate was last passed. Site-specific changes are announced in operations bulletins.

## Forklift certification
Forklift operators must hold a valid forklift certificate. The standard recertification interval is 24 months. Operators who work night shifts must also complete the low-light module once a year.

## Cold storage induction
Anyone working in a cold storage area, including visitors staying more than 2 hours, must complete the cold storage induction before their first shift. The induction is valid for 12 months.

## First aid
Each shift must have at least one first-aider on site. Shift managers and site directors must hold a first aid certificate, renewed every 36 months.
