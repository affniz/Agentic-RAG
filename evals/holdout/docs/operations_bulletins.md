# Operations Bulletins

Bulletins issued by the VP of Operations. A bulletin overrides the policy documents and the sites list from its effective date.

## 2026-03-10 - Lyon band change
Lyon Crossdock moves from allowance band B to band A, effective 2026-05-01, after its night-shift volume dropped.

## 2026-06-18 - Reno forklift recertification
Because of a rise in cold-store incidents, forklift operators at Reno Cold Store must recertify every 6 months instead of the standard interval, effective 2026-07-01. This applies to existing certificates too: they expire 6 months after the date they were passed.

## 2026-09-02 - Weekend cap for Hamilton
Weekend overtime at Hamilton Yard is capped at 8 hours per employee per weekend, effective 2026-09-15, while the yard is resurfaced. The cap ends on 2027-01-31.

## 2026-10-20 - Leipzig peak season
For the peak season from 2026-11-15 to 2027-01-10, Leipzig Hub overtime is paid at 2.0x on all shifts, including weekdays.
