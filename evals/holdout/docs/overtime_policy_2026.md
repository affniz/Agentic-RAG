# Overtime and Shift Allowance Policy 2026

Status: CURRENT. Effective 2026-04-01; replaces the 2025 policy.

## Overtime rates
Weekday overtime is paid at 1.5x the base hourly rate. Weekend overtime is paid at 2.0x. Public holiday work is paid at 2.5x.

## Night shift allowance
Night shifts (22:00-06:00) earn a flat allowance per shift, set by the site's allowance band:
- Band A: $20 per night shift
- Band B: $28 per night shift
- Band C: $36 per night shift

## Approval
Overtime above 12 hours in a week needs the site director's approval, recorded in the rostering system.

## Cold storage supplement
Employees working in cold storage areas earn a supplement of $3 per hour on top of overtime pay. The supplement does not apply to office staff at cold storage sites.
