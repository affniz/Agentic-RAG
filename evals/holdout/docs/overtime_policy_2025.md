# Overtime and Shift Allowance Policy 2025

Status: SUPERSEDED. In force from 2025-01-01 until 2026-03-31; replaced by the 2026 policy from 2026-04-01.

## Overtime rates
Weekday overtime is paid at 1.5x the base hourly rate. Weekend overtime is paid at 1.75x. Public holiday work is paid at 2.0x.

## Night shift allowance
Night shifts (22:00-06:00) earn a flat allowance per shift, set by the site's allowance band:
- Band A: $18 per night shift
- Band B: $24 per night shift
- Band C: $30 per night shift

## Approval
Overtime above 10 hours in a week needs the shift manager's written approval.
