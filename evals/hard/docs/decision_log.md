# Executive Decision Log

Decisions recorded by the executive team. Where a decision conflicts with a policy document, the decision takes precedence from its effective date.

## 2026-04-20 - Design laptop refresh cycle
Laptops for the Design department move to a 3-year refresh cycle, the same as Engineering, effective immediately. Rationale: design tools now need GPU performance comparable to engineering workloads.

## 2026-05-03 - Lisbon office closure
The Lisbon office will close on 2026-12-31 when its lease ends. Lisbon-based employees may work remotely from Portugal or relocate to the Berlin office, with relocation support.

## 2026-08-12 - Berlin cost tier
The Berlin office is reclassified from Tier 2 to Tier 1, effective 2026-09-01, after a review of local hotel and rental prices.

## 2026-10-02 - Q1 2027 travel freeze
Discretionary travel is frozen from 2027-01-01 to 2027-03-31. Only customer visits are exempt, and each customer visit must be approved by the traveller's department VP. Internal offsites, team visits and conferences are not exempt.
