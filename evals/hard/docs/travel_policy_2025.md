# Travel Policy 2025 (employees)

Status: SUPERSEDED. This policy was in force from 2025-01-01 until 2026-02-28 and has been replaced by the 2026 Travel Policy.

## Hotels
Nightly hotel caps depend on the cost tier of the destination office:
- Tier 1: $320 per night
- Tier 2: $220 per night
- Tier 3: $150 per night

## Per diem
Daily meal allowance by destination region: AMER $70, EMEA $80, APAC $65.

## Flights
Economy class by default. Premium economy is allowed for flights of 6 hours or longer.
