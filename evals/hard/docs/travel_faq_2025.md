# Travel FAQ

Last updated: January 2025. Questions people ask People Ops about travel.

**What's the hotel cap in London?**
London is a Tier 1 city, so the cap is $320 per night.

**Can I fly premium economy to Tokyo?**
Yes. Flights to Tokyo from any of our other offices are longer than 6 hours, so premium economy is allowed.

**Do I need approval for business class?**
Business class is not allowed.

**How fast do I need to submit expenses?**
Within 30 days of returning.
