# Amendment to the 2026 Travel Policy (June 2026)

Approved by the executive team on 2026-06-10. Effective 2026-07-01. All other provisions of the 2026 Travel Policy remain unchanged.

1. The nightly hotel cap for Tier 1 destinations is raised to $400 per night, reflecting hotel price increases in Tier 1 cities. Tier 2 and Tier 3 caps are unchanged.
2. The minimum flight duration for premium economy returns to 6 hours.
