# Pricing and Discounts 2026

## Plans
- Starter: $500 per month, up to 25 robots.
- Growth: $2,000 per month, up to 200 robots.
- Enterprise: custom pricing, starting at $8,000 per month.

## Discounts
- Annual prepay: 15% off the list price.
- Multi-year (3-year commitment): 25% off the list price.
The annual prepay and multi-year discounts cannot be combined.

## Approvals
Account executives may offer discounts of up to 20% without approval. Any discount above 20% requires approval from the VP Sales.
