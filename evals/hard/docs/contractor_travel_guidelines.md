# Contractor Travel Guidelines

These guidelines apply only to contractors and agency staff. Employees follow the Travel Policy.

Contractors must have travel pre-approved in writing by the hiring manager.
Contractor hotel caps: $250 per night in Tier 1 cities, $180 per night in Tier 2 cities, $120 per night in Tier 3 cities.
Contractors receive a flat per diem of $50 in all regions. Contractors always fly economy.
