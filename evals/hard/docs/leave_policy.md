# Leave Policy

Applies to all employees. Country appendices override this policy where they say so.

## Annual vacation
Employees at levels L3-L6 receive 20 vacation days per year. Employees at L7 and above receive 23 days per year.
Employees earn 1 additional day for each full year of service beyond 3 years, up to a maximum of 5 additional days.
Tenure is counted in full years of service as of January 1 of the calendar year.

## Carry-over
Up to 5 unused vacation days may be carried into the next calendar year. Carried-over days expire on March 31.

## Sabbatical
After 5 full years of service, employees may take a 4-week paid sabbatical, once in each 5-year period.
Sabbaticals must be requested at least 3 months in advance and approved by your department VP.
