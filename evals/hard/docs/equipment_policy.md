# Equipment and Purchasing Policy

## Laptop refresh
Engineering laptops are refreshed every 3 years. Laptops for all other departments are refreshed every 4 years.
The refresh date is counted from the date the current laptop was issued.

## Monitors
Every employee may expense one external monitor. Employees in Design and in the Perception team may expense a second monitor.

## Purchase approvals
Approval depends on the total cost of the purchase:
- Up to $2,000: the employee's manager.
- Over $2,000 and up to $10,000: the director of the employee's department.
- Over $10,000: the employee's department VP and the Finance Director, both required.
Purchases must not be split to stay under a threshold.
