# Travel Policy 2026 (employees)

Effective 2026-03-01. Supersedes the 2025 Travel Policy. Applies to all Halcyon employees; contractors follow the separate contractor travel guidelines.

## Hotels
The nightly hotel cap depends on the cost tier of the destination office:
- Tier 1: $360 per night
- Tier 2: $240 per night
- Tier 3: $160 per night

For destination cities where Halcyon has no office, the Tier 2 cap applies.
For stays longer than 14 consecutive nights, the cap is reduced by 20% from the 15th night onward.

## Per diem
Daily meal allowance by destination region: AMER $75, EMEA $85, APAC $70.

## Flights
Economy class by default. Premium economy is allowed for flights of 7 hours or longer.
Business class is allowed only for flights of 12 hours or longer, and only with approval from your department VP.

## Booking and expenses
Book flights and hotels through the travel portal at least 14 days in advance where possible.
Submit expense reports within 30 days of returning. Receipts are required for any single expense over $40.
