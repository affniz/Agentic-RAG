# Engineering Handbook

## Code review
Every change needs one approving review. Changes to the Fleet API also need a review from the Cloud team.

## Deploys
Production deploys run Monday to Thursday, 09:00-16:00 in the deploying engineer's time zone. No deploys on Fridays or during company holidays.

## Hardware lab
The robotics hardware lab is in the Lisbon office. Booking is through the lab calendar; sessions are limited to 4 hours.

## Travel for engineers
Engineers travelling to another office for team work follow the employee Travel Policy. Plan visits at least 3 weeks ahead.
